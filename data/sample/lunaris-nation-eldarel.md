## **Eldarel**

*4 Elements, 4 Seasons, 4 Keepers, and the 4 Gods*  
Eastern Half of the Central Continent

Eldarel is a land of the Eldaren no longer. The continent has fallen under the control of five elemental Wilderkin groups known as the Ildarim: the Oreads, Ifrits, Sylphs, Undines, and Taloses. These beings, claiming to be gods of specific natural features: such as particular rocks, rivers, or forests, assert their power based on their strength, with the most powerful Ildarim reigning over the grandest locations.

Though the Ildarim allow the Eldaren to exist, this is only out of respect for the ancient Pantheon of Seasons. The Eldaren must live under the oppressive rules and pacts set by the Ildarim, knowing that any breach of these agreements brings brutal consequences, from centuries of servitude to torturous decades of suffering.

Despite their supposed respect for the Pantheon, the Ildarim have seized the sacred domains of the Keepers, turning these holy sites into their own territories. This has created a dire situation for the Eldaren, as their rituals, such as the Rite of Cleansing, must be performed in these now-occupied lands. Some Eldaren, driven by faith and desperation, willingly break the Ildarim’s rules, sacrificing themselves in futile attempts to carry out these vital rites.

### **Governance**

Eldarel’s governance is structured as a democratic oligarchy with a strong theocratic influence, creating a unique and intricate system of power. The land is overseen by five Arbiters, each voted on by the respective churches dedicated to the gods of the Pantheon of Seasons: Alari, Asta, Aradan, and Ailor. These Arbiters hold significant sway, not just over their churches, but over the cities and regions where their influence reigns. One Arbiter represents the will of the Eldaren people, chosen not by divine influence but through democratic processes. This creates a delicate balance between religious authority and the voice of the populace.

The heart of this governance is in Anlor, the capital, which uniquely operates outside the control of any church. Here, true democracy is practiced, allowing the Eldaren people a space where their voices are unfiltered by theocratic influence. However, even in Anlor, the omnipresent influence of rituals and the ever-looming presence of the Ildarim cannot be ignored. These rituals are central to life in Eldarel, both as acts of worship to the Pantheon and as necessary appeasements to the Ildarim who now claim dominion over the continent’s natural features.

The governance of Eldarel is thus a complex dance between democracy, oligarchy, and theocratic rule, with the ever-present undercurrent of ancient, elemental powers that both respect and threaten the fragile order maintained by the Arbiters.

The Ildarim enforce these pacts with unyielding strictness, ensuring that the Eldaren never forget their place beneath the elemental beings who now rule their ancestral lands. The origins of the Ten Bindings are shrouded in the bitter history of Eldarel’s fall during the Fracturing and the Age of Strife. After the Ildarim claimed the continent, they sought to solidify their dominance not just through brute force but through a systematic breaking of the Eldaren spirit. These pacts were born out of the Ildarim's disdain for the Eldaren's former sovereignty. Each binding is a chain, linking the Eldaren to a life of servitude and fear, and any attempt to break these chains is met with severe punishment. Despite this, some Eldaren hold onto the faint hope that one day, the bindings will be shattered, and their people will reclaim their land and freedom.

### **Worship**

**Pantheon of Seasons**  
*The four seasons, essences, and elements.*

In Eldaren belief, the Pantheon of Seasons: composed of the quadruplet deities Alari, Asta, Aradan, and Ailo, was born from the magic of the moon, their divine breath giving life to Lunaris itself. Each deity embodies a fundamental essence of reality, overseeing an element of the realm and governing the shifting of seasons. These gods are revered as the guardians of the natural balance, their powers intricately woven into the very fabric of existence on Lunaris. The Eldaren hold that these divine beings collectively guide the realm’s movements, supported by their devoted Keepers, who assist in overseeing the natural order and maintaining the gods’ influence.

During the Fracturing, the Eldaren were thrown into disarray as the gods’ presence suddenly fell silent, and the moon,a symbol of divine power, disappeared from the sky. In this void, the Keepers emerged during some eras before and the Eldaren began to believe that these enigmatic beings were the manifestations of the gods themselves, continuing their work in maintaining the balance of Lunaris. The Keepers became the central figures of worship, taking on the roles that the Pantheon had once filled.

However, when the moon eventually returned as a pale, lifeless satellite, the Eldaren found themselves divided. A schism formed within their beliefs: some held to the faith that the gods had returned with the moon, viewing it as a sign of their continued presence. Others, however, saw the Keepers as the true embodiments of the Pantheon, believing that the gods had passed their mantle onto these beings to guide Lunaris in their stead. This division has persisted, creating a complex and sometimes contentious spiritual landscape among the Eldaren.

### **Languages**

* **Eldari**  
  Script: Eldari  
  Eldari is the common language spoken by the elves of Eldarel. It is a melodious and expressive language, reflecting the elves' deep connection to nature and magic.

* **Aqueldari**  
  Script: Aqueldari  
  Aqueldari is the tactile version of Eldari, adapted for underwater use. It employs a system of touch and subtle hand movements to convey meaning, allowing communication even in the depths of the ocean.

* **Ildari**  
  Script: Eldari  
  Ildari is the ritualistic language used to communicate with the Ildarim, the spirits and mystical beings of Eldarel. It is a language steeped in tradition and mysticism, used in rituals and sacred ceremonies.

### **Scripts**

* **Eldari Script**  
  Eldari script is characterized by its flowing, elegant lines, suitable for carving into wood and stone. The curves and fluidity of the script reflect the Elves' appreciation for beauty and harmony in their writing. The script's elegance is intended to complement the natural materials it is often inscribed upon.

* **Aqueldari Script**  
  Adapted from Eldari for underwater use, Aqueldari script is designed to be read by touch. Characters are inscribed on shells and stones with raised, fluid shapes that mimic the movement of water. This tactile design allows aquatic creatures to interpret the script by feeling it, making it both functional and beautiful in the underwater environment.

* **Astani Script**  
  Used in Eldarel for magical notation during schooling, the Astani script features precise geometric shapes and patterns. Used to record magic, its structures, and core foci, the script’s precision ensures accurate transcription and understanding of magical knowledge. The use of parchment or specially prepared surfaces allows for the exact rendering of its intricate designs, which are essential for the proper practice of arcane arts.
