# **History📘**

## **Timeline and Eras**
It is unknown how Lunaris came to be nor how old it is. Scholars have no resource to piece together this mystery or even gain a sense of semblance on the matter. The world is aged along with its many constants, it is best to accept this. 

**Faded Epoch**  
The Faded Epoch on Lunaris is shrouded in mystery, characterized by the complete absence of traceable records of civilizations and their unfolding events. It is an era where knowledge and history are lost in the sands of time. Scholars and historians have nothing to piece together the era before the Age of Alchemy, with nothing to unlock the secrets of how society came to be.

**Vashtai Conquest**  
The Vashtai Conquest is a historical event regarding Fargosh’s conquest on the Agrashan Islands. Although no contemporary records from the Vashtai Conquest have been found in the modern age, references to it exist from the Age of Alchemy. These fragments suggest a period of intense conflict and transformation in the region, but the full details remain elusive.

**Age of Alchemy**  
	Knowledge of the Age of Alchemy comes primarily from a bronze plate, known as a 'Holel,' used for recording news in Fargosh. Currently stored in the Academy of Arclight in Astani, Eldarel, this Holel describes how the end of the Vashtai Conquest led to the flourishing of alchemical practices when the practice of alchemy was no longer kept a secret. The influx of rich scholars from other nations transformed the city of Fetvine into a hub of alchemical knowledge and innovation. Two other Holels from this era have yet to be deciphered by Asterinian scholars, leaving more to be discovered about this pivotal age.

**The Fracturing**  
*Specific effects of the fracturing will be expounded upon on the civilizations portion of the document.* 

	Little is known about what truly caused the Fracturing, but its consequences were cataclysmic, marking the end of times and the dawn of unrelenting darkness. Three pivotal events define this age: the gods fell silent, their divine presence withdrawing from the world; nature, once a formidable force, became an unquestioned and tyrannical authority over all life; and the Arcane, once the beacon of magical prowess, faded from existence, abandoned by most. 

The first sign of the Fracturing was the sudden and inexplicable silence of the gods and the disappearing of the moon. For as long as anyone could remember, the gods had been a guiding force in the world, their will manifesting through miracles, divine magic, and the clear guidance of their faithful. But then, without warning, their voices were silenced. Priests and clerics who once channeled divine power found themselves cut off, their prayers unanswered and their holy rites powerless. The faithful were left adrift, searching for meaning in a world that seemed to have been abandoned by the very beings they worshiped.

In the void left by the gods, nature seized control with a brutal and unyielding hand. Once a force to be reckoned with, nature's power grew exponentially, becoming an unparalleled and oppressive force over the realm. Forests expanded into once-thriving cities and rivers broke free of their banks to carve new paths through the land. The Primal Face of nature, wild and unforgiving, became the new law of the land, while the Wilted Face spread its corruption, turning fertile fields into desolate wastelands. Humanity and other civilized races found themselves at the mercy of a world that no longer had any space for them, forced to adapt or be swallowed whole by the relentless growth and decay of nature.

The final blow of the Fracturing came with the transformation of magic from a reliable tool into a wild and treacherous force. The Arcane was described to be unruly and inconsistent, almost as if it no longer wanted to be brought to reality. What was once a cornerstone of civilization, used to build, heal, and protect, became erratic and perilous. This unpredictability bred fear and distrust, prompting societies to deem the practice of magic too dangerous to tolerate. In this new age, the art of magic became a taboo, its once-celebrated wonders overshadowed by the chaos and destruction it now wrought.

**Age of Strife**  
The Age of Strife marked a dark chapter in the history of Lunaris, as the lands that once flourished under the gods’ favor twisted under the merciless grip of nature. Though not all lands were overtaken by this creeping wilderness, the safe havens that remained were few and far between. Fertile fields, verdant forests, and prosperous towns became rare treasures, coveted by all who sought to survive in a world where nature’s wrath could turn even the most secure stronghold into a wasteland. The nations of Lunaris, desperate to secure these precious territories, turned on one another in a brutal struggle for survival.

By this time, magic had returned to a state of stability, its chaotic nature tempered once more. However, civilizations across Lunaris had come to distrust and disdain its use. The Fracturing had left deep scars, and the horrors of uncontrolled magic were still fresh in collective memory. Each nation, for its own reasons, chose to turn away from the arcane. Some saw magic as a relic of a bygone age, too dangerous and unpredictable to rely upon; others believed that the wielding of such power was an affront to the natural order, inviting disaster. And so, even as magic once again became safe, it was largely forsaken as a tool.

The moon, which had vanished during the Fracturing, returned to the skies of Lunaris during the Age of Strife. But it was no longer the radiant blue orb that had once guided sailors and blessed the harvests. Instead, it was a pale gray, a haunting specter in the night sky, casting a cold, ashen light over the warring lands below. Its return was seen by some as an omen of worse things to come, a silent witness to the bloodshed and despair that now defined the realm. As nations clashed, the moon’s ghostly glow illuminated the battlefields where brother fought brother, and neighbor turned on neighbor, all in a futile struggle to claim what little remained.

The conflicts of the Age of Strife were between powerful nations, each vying for control over the dwindling pockets of land still free from nature’s grasp. Castles were besieged, cities were razed, and countless lives were lost as kings and queens sought to expand their domains at the expense of their neighbors. The battles were fierce, and the stakes were high, for in a world where nature had turned hostile, the few remaining bastions of safety were worth any price in blood. The moon's ashen glow cast long shadows over these grim wars, where the once-great civilizations of Lunaris fought not just for power, but for the very right to survive.

The nations of Lunaris decided to end the war once Fargosh fractured, literally, under the weight of the war and the weight of keeping their own afloat. Its continent reduced to an echo of what it used to be. Those that remain afloat are enveloped by the Primal while the rest are lost forever to the mist. 

**Age of Resurgence**  
An era marked by a tentative and uneasy peace as the war-weary nations of Lunaris step back from the brink of further conflict to lick their wounds and rebuild their shattered lands. After half a millennia of relentless warfare, the leaders of each realm, recognizing the unsustainable toll on their people and resources, agree on a 100-year peace treaty. This truce, though fragile, offers a glimmer of hope and a respite from the horrors of war.

During this period, societies shift their focus from destruction to recovery. Cities and villages rise from the ashes, their streets bustling with the activity of reconstruction. Trade routes, once perilous and desolate, reopen, and the exchange of goods, knowledge, and culture flourishes anew. Farmers return to their fields, artisans to their crafts, and scholars to their studies, all striving to restore what was lost and to innovate for the future.

Yet, amidst the efforts to heal and rebuild, a pervasive sense of impermanence lingers. The specter of war looms large, and the knowledge that the peace treaty is but a temporary reprieve weighs heavily on the minds of all. This delicate balance between hope and fear defines the Age of Resurgence, a time of cautious optimism and relentless preparation for whatever the future may hold.

## **Current Day**

Seven years after the end of the signed peace treaty, the world of Lunaris finds itself in a precarious equilibrium. The initial relief that followed the cessation of hostilities has given way to a persistent undercurrent of tension, as the memory of war lingers like a shadow over the land. While no war is officially declared, the signs of mounting discord are unmistakable.

In this seventh year after the treaty, the world stands at a crossroads. The uneasy peace holds, but for how long is uncertain. The leaders and peoples of Lunaris are faced with a choice: to continue striving for a lasting peace or to succumb to the inexorable pull of war. The future hangs in the balance, shaped by the actions and decisions of those living in this pivotal moment.

## **Calendar**

The calendar of Lunaris, crafted by the Eladarel, spans 360 days divided into 12 months of 30 days each. Each month is organized into 6 weeks, known as pentads, with each pentad consisting of 5 days. This system was designed to synchronize with the natural cycles and seasons, providing a structured and predictable framework for daily life, agriculture, and festivals in Lunaris.
